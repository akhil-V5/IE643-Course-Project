PlantSeg Disease-Weighted ResShift Training Package

Independently authored integration code and documentation for a research course
project. Not an official PlantSeg or ResShift release. No model weights, dataset
images, third-party repository source, or font files are redistributed here.
The bootstrap downloads official ResShift resources separately. Their licenses
remain applicable; read external/ResShift/LICENSE before use or redistribution.
PlantSeg source images may have individual licenses.

No warranty of model accuracy, disease diagnosis, or compatibility with all
hardware/software combinations. Tests and untested components are documented
in docs/TEST_REPORT.md. The model has not been trained on the user's dataset in
this environment. The proposed lesion-weighted objective requires experimental
validation, including false-symptom and lesion-erasure tests.
